Request a studio visit

Invite me over for a day. I’ll make a short film about the work, the people, and the things that don’t make it into a portfolio.

What happens

I spend at least half a day with you. We talk, I film the space, the work, and whatever’s on the desks. Then I cut it into a short film for your channels and mine.

What you need to give me

Half a day minimum, and someone who can talk about the work. That’s it. You don’t need to tidy up.
