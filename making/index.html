The portfolio, in five states.

The same six projects and the same words, held five different ways over a month. Each one below is the real build from that day, running live. Click one to open it full size.

  1. 00Parts16 Aug

    Before any code: a brief and a folder of Figma exports. Six pills still named Frame 608 to 613, one sheet of speech per project, a favicon. Everything the site would be, in pieces.
  2. 01Sketch16 Aug

    First pass. Six pills on a ring, speech bubbles exported from Figma as SVG, grey tiles standing in for the work. The bubbles were too big and the lines ran straight through them.
  3. 02Vector18 Aug

    The ring learned to fit the screen and park the others in a dome. Real thumbnails, a footer, the SVG bubbles rescaled. It looked right and it lagged, because every bubble carried a blur filter.
  4. 03Typed30 Aug

    The bubbles stopped being pictures and became text that types itself. White ground, dot grid, tethers that colour on hover. The first version that felt like a conversation.
  5. 04Shipped14 Sep

    A phone layout of its own, pills you can drag, the studio visit card, and a hundred small fixes: tether joins, bubble widths, animations that run at the same speed on every screen.
